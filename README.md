# Ecommerce Product Research System

A practical ecommerce product research dashboard for analyzing products, suppliers, profit potential, demand level, competition level, risk level, and listing ideas.

## Project Overview

This project was built as part of my ecommerce, AI automation, and product research portfolio. The goal was to create a structured dashboard that helps analyze product opportunities before listing them on an ecommerce marketplace.

The dashboard takes product and supplier details as input, then generates a basic product research analysis using simple business logic.

The dashboard takes these inputs:

* Product name
* Supplier name
* Product cost price
* Expected selling price
* Shipping cost
* Demand level
* Competition level
* Risk notes

Based on these inputs, it generates:

* Estimated profit
* Profit margin
* Product score
* Risk level
* Research summary
* Listing title idea
* Final product decision

## Example Use Case

An ecommerce seller researching a product from TeemDrop can use this dashboard to quickly check estimated profit, margin, demand level, competition level, and possible product risk before listing the product.

Example input:

* Product name: Portable Blender
* Supplier name: TeemDrop
* Product cost price: $14
* Expected selling price: $39
* Shipping cost: $5
* Demand level: High
* Competition level: Medium
* Risk notes: Need to verify supplier shipping time, product quality, stock availability, and marketplace policy before listing.

The dashboard then creates a structured research summary, listing title idea, product score, risk level, and final decision.
